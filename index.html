      About the conversion mapping (and why seams can shift)

      Solid colors and designs painted separately per panel convert cleanly. Artwork that's painted continuously across panel edges — a hood shape, a print that wraps from the front onto the sleeves, fabric folds crossing a seam — will look shifted or "broken" at those edges after conversion. This isn't a bug: Roblox's classic template and Polytoria's template don't place matching panels at the same relative spacing, so pixel-perfect continuity across a panel boundary can't be preserved by any converter, including the reference tools this mapping was verified against. Each individual panel still lands in the geometrically correct spot — only art that spans two panels will show a seam.
